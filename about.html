About Me

Software Engineer & Tech Founder ⚡

I'm Samuel Chima, a Software Engineering student at the African Leadership University in Kigali. I hold a BA in Philosophy and have about three years of experience at digital agencies, where I worked on websites, SEO, and digital marketing.

In finance, I research equities and African tech investment, with the goal of a career at the intersection of venture capital, equity research, and the African tech ecosystem.
I am also proud to serve as a Study Mentor at ALX Africa, where I contribute to training students across the continent in Artificial Intelligence and essential career skills, equipping them to become future leaders in the tech industry.
